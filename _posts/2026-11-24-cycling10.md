*10 years of cycling*

## What happens if you got to the physiotherapist?

In 2016 I got injured during some futsal session with friends. Nothing serious, just adduction strain. I had to rest, but I wanted to do some activities. The physiotherapist told me to swim (not for me) or to do some cycling. After 10 years I haven't yet play futsal again, but I discovered that cycling is not that bad after all.
